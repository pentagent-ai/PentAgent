Terms

The short, plain-English terms for using PentAgent and this website.

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 18 October 2026

Who we are

PentAgent is made by [Entity name]. By using the app or this site, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. You need to be 18 or older.

The pilot

PentAgent is in an invite-only pilot. Things will change, and some features may not work perfectly yet. We may pause or end pilot access, and we’ll let you know when we can. Please keep your sign-in to yourself.

Only test what you're allowed to

Only test apps you own or have permission to test. Stay within the scope you’ve agreed with the owner, and follow the laws where you and the target are. You’re responsible for what you test with PentAgent.

Your AI model and your key

  • You connect your own model: a provider with your API key, or a local or self-hosted model.
  • The provider sees the traffic you send it, including requests, responses and any tokens or passwords inside them. PentAgent doesn’t mask or hide anything.
  • For sensitive targets, a local model keeps everything on your machine.
  • Model costs are between you and your provider. You can set a spend cap; it’s off by default.

You stay in control

In Agent mode, every live request waits for your approval before it’s sent, and the Stop button ends a run at any time. Please review what you approve.

Your work is yours

Your projects, captured traffic and findings stay on your computer and belong to you. The app itself belongs to us: please don’t copy, resell or reverse-engineer it.

No guarantees

PentAgent is pilot software, provided as it is. It can have bugs and can miss issues, and AI output can be wrong, so please double-check findings before you rely on them.

Changes and contact

If we update these terms, we’ll post the new version here and the app will ask you to accept it.
