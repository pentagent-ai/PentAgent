      Sign in

      Sign in with GitHub and what happens while your access is being approved.

      PentAgent asks you to sign in each time it can’t find a saved session, for example on the first launch or after you sign out. Signing in lets PentAgent confirm that your account is part of the pilot.

      Sign in with GitHub#

      1. Choose Continue with GitHub

        Click Continue with GitHub. The button changes to Redirecting… while GitHub opens.

      2. Approve on GitHub

        Sign in to GitHub if you aren’t already, and approve PentAgent. Use the GitHub account from your pilot request, since that’s the one on the approved list.

      3. Back to PentAgent

        GitHub sends you back to PentAgent, which checks your access. If you’re approved and it’s your first time, you’ll see the welcome screen next. After that, PentAgent opens straight to your workspace.

      What PentAgent gets from GitHub

      Your name, GitHub username, email address and profile picture. PentAgent uses them to check your access and to show who’s signed in. It doesn’t ask for access to your repositories.

      Email sign-in is coming soon

      Signing in with a one-time code sent to your email is on its way. For now, use GitHub.

      How access checks work#

      Right after you sign in, PentAgent asks our server whether your account is on the pilot list. It checks again every few hours while the app is open, and whenever you come back to the PentAgent window.

      If you lose your internet connection, you can keep working. As long as PentAgent confirmed your access on this computer within the last three days, it lets you in with the same account.

      If your access isn’t approved yet#

      If your account isn’t on the pilot list yet, PentAgent shows Not approved yet with the GitHub username it checked.

      What you can do:

      • Haven’t applied yet? Click Apply for pilot access to open the pilot request form.
      • Already applied? We review requests by hand, so it can take a little while. Once you’re approved, open PentAgent again and you’ll go straight in.
      • Signed in with a different GitHub account? Click Sign out and sign in with the account from your request.

      Other screens you might see#

      Could not verify pilot access#

      PentAgent couldn’t reach our server to check your access, and there’s no recent confirmation on this computer to fall back on. Check your connection, then click Try again. You can also Sign out from here.

      Update required#

      Your build is older than the oldest version the pilot supports. Click Get the latest build, then follow Updating to a new build.

      When a newer build is out but yours still works, PentAgent shows a small Update available bar at the top of the window instead. It has a Download link and a Dismiss button, so you can update when it suits you.

      Your account menu#

      Once you’re in, your account button sits at the far right of the top bar. It shows your GitHub profile picture, or your initials if you don’t have one. Click it to see your account details.

      1. 1Marker 1: Your name and GitHub username.
      2. 2Marker 2: The email address on your GitHub account.
      3. 3Marker 3: Your access status, for example Pilot access · allowed.
      4. 4Marker 4: Sign out.

      The menu only ever shows your own details. Press Esc or click anywhere else to close it.

      Signing out#

      Open the account menu and click Sign out. PentAgent returns to the sign-in screen. Your projects and captured traffic stay on your computer and are there when you sign in again.

      If sign-in doesn’t work#

      What you seeWhat to do
      GitHub opens but you never get back to PentAgentClose the GitHub tab, go back to PentAgent and click Continue with GitHub again. Make sure you finish the approval on GitHub.
      Not approved yet, but you were approvedCheck you signed in with the GitHub account from your pilot request. If it's the right one, sign out and sign in again.
      Could not verify pilot accessCheck your internet connection, then click Try again.
