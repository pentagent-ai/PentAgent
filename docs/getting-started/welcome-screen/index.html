      Welcome screen

      The two quick checkboxes you see once, before the app opens.

      The first time you sign in, PentAgent shows a short welcome screen before the app opens. It asks for two things, and you only need to do it once.

      What’s on the screen#

      1. 1Marker 1: I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy. The two links open the Terms and Privacy Policy on our website, so you can read them first.
      2. 2Marker 2: I’ll only test apps I own or have permission to test. A simple promise that keeps everyone safe.
      3. 3Marker 3: Includes anonymous usage analytics. The link opens the analytics section of the Privacy Policy.
      4. 4Marker 4: The versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy you’re agreeing to.
      5. 5Marker 5: Continue becomes available once both boxes are ticked.

      Tick both boxes and continue#

      Tick both boxes and click Continue. The button shows Saving… for a moment while PentAgent records your choice on your account, then the app opens.

      Prefer to stop here? Click Sign out. Nothing is saved, and you’ll see this screen again next time you sign in.

      About analytics#

      PentAgent sends anonymous usage analytics, such as which features get used, to help us improve it. Analytics only start once you’ve clicked Continue. Nothing is sent from the sign-in or welcome screens.

      Your captured traffic stays on your computer. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is and isn’t collected.

      When you’ll see it again#

      Your answer is saved to your account, not just this computer, so signing in elsewhere won’t ask again. You’ll only see the welcome screen again if we publish a new version of the Terms or the Privacy Policy. In that case, have a quick look at what changed and tick the boxes again.

      If it doesn’t work#

      If PentAgent can’t load the current Terms and Privacy versions, it shows We couldn’t load the latest terms with a Try again button instead of the checkboxes.

      Here’s what each message means:

      MessageWhat to do
      You're offline. Reconnect and try again.Check your internet connection, then click Try again.
      We couldn't reach PentAgent just now. Try again in a moment.Wait a moment, then click Try again.
      We're still setting things up on our side. Try again a little later.Nothing's wrong on your end. Try again a little later.
      We couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again.Check your connection, then click Try again.
      That didn't save. Please try again.Click Try again.
      The Terms or Privacy Policy just changed. Have a quick look and tick the boxes again.A new version came out while the screen was open. Read it, tick both boxes and continue.
      You've been signed out. Sign in again to continue.Sign in again with GitHub.

      Note

      PentAgent only opens once your choice is safely saved, so if a message appears you’ll stay on this screen until it works.
