Privacy Policy

Your traffic, projects and keys stay on your computer. Here's the little we do collect, and why.

Version 1.1.0 · Effective 18 October 2026

What stays on your computer

Captured traffic, your projects and findings, your API keys and the local proxy certificate are stored on your machine. We never receive them.

Your AI model

When you use Ask, Plan or Agent, the relevant traffic goes straight from your computer to the model you connected. It doesn’t pass through us. The provider sees it as captured, with nothing masked, and handles it under its own privacy policy. A local model keeps it all on your machine. More in our Terms.

Analytics

Anonymous usage analytics are part of using PentAgent. After you accept these terms, the app sends usage events to PostHog (hosted in the EU) under a random install ID. They help us see what’s used and fix problems, and they aren’t linked to your account.

What we track

  • feature and screen usage, like “Repeater request sent” or “map viewed”
  • session length and how often you use the app
  • app version, browser and operating system
  • screen size and timezone
  • the random install ID
  • error reports, with URLs, emails and tokens removed

What we never track

  • captured traffic, requests or responses
  • target URLs or anything about what you test
  • your prompts or the AI’s output
  • API keys, tokens or other credentials
  • your email or GitHub username
  • anything else that identifies you

Analytics can’t be turned off in the app. If you’d rather not share them, you can stop using PentAgent, and collection stops when you do. There’s no session recording or click tracking.

Your account and pilot request

To run sign-in and the pilot, we keep a few records (stored with Supabase):

  • your GitHub sign-in details, such as your username and email
  • whether you’re in the pilot
  • which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and when
  • what you enter in the pilot request form, used only to review your request and get in touch

We don’t sell your data or use it for ads. The services that help us are Supabase (sign-in and records), PostHog (analytics) and GitHub (sign-in).

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We’ll post a contact address here soon. PentAgent is for people 18 and over.
